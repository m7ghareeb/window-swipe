The first public release of **Window Swipe**, a Cinnamon extension: swipe horizontally with three
or four fingers to slide between the windows of the current workspace.

![Window Swipe demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/m7ghareeb/window-swipe/v1.0.0/docs/images/demo.gif)

## Highlights

- The window follows your fingers and glides into place with a smooth, macOS-inspired animation
  that starts at your fingers' speed, with no jump and no overshoot.
- Three- and four-finger swipes; rapid swipes continue smoothly from where the windows are.
- Animation profiles: Responsive, Relaxed, **Calm** (default), Gentle.
- Switching sensitivity: **Balanced** (default), Sensitive, Less sensitive, Flick-friendly, Deliberate.
- Window order follows the Plank dock, falling back to window opening order.
- Minimized, maximized and fullscreen windows and the desktop are supported.
- Native Cinnamon settings window; changes apply from the next swipe, with no restart.

## Installation

1. Download `window-swipe@ghareeb-v1.0.0.zip` below.
2. `unzip window-swipe@ghareeb-v1.0.0.zip -d ~/.local/share/cinnamon/extensions/`
3. Enable **Window Swipe** in **System Settings → Extensions**.

Gestures must be enabled in **System Settings → Gestures**, with an action assigned to swipe
left and swipe right with three and four fingers (any action; Window Swipe takes these swipes
over). Verify the download with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`.

Window Swipe is not yet in the Cinnamon Spices catalogue; that submission is still to come.

## Compatibility

Tested on Linux Mint 22.3 with Cinnamon 6.6.9 (X11), Touchégg 2.0.16, on a single monitor.

## Known limitations

- Within one swipe the slide can't pass the starting window to the other side (Touchégg locks a
  swipe's direction when it starts). Lift your fingers and swipe again.
- Only the current workspace and the monitor of the starting window are included; multi-monitor
  setups are untested.
